**Note:** When interpreting with context, the interpreter considers the landscape within and without the sample unit and applies the land use definitions to the patterns in the landscape. The plot locations in this figure are the same as in Figure 12. Just as in Figure 12, the sample unit is 2 ha in size and the forest definition requires canopy cover greater than or equal to 60 percent with a minimum map unit of 2 ha. In the upper figures, the land use within the sample unit is a mix of grassland (24 percent), cropland (40 percent), and forest (36 percent). In the lower figures, the patch of trees does not reach 2 ha; therefore, the land use is considered grassland. Note how different the land use calls are for interpretation with versus without context (compare to Figure 12).
